# MHD energy principle conventions

The MHD energy principle, due to Friedberg and Bernstein, defines plasma
stability in terms of the potential energy functional δW.

## Core definition

**δW > 0 → STABLE**

δW is the potential energy associated with a Lagrangian displacement ξ of the
plasma from equilibrium. If displacing the plasma *raises* the total potential
energy, the equilibrium is linearly stable (the equilibrium is a local minimum
of the energy functional).

## Mercier criterion

The local Mercier criterion for interchange stability:

D_M > 0 → stable (positive in most standard formulations).

D_M is derived from δW through a minimization procedure over short-wavelength
modes localized on a flux surface.

## Suydam criterion

A necessary condition for local stability in a cylindrical pinch: stability
holds when the local Suydam index is satisfied. The Suydam index follows from
the δW formulation applied to a 1D cylindrical equilibrium.

## Sign convention warning

Some European and Russian texts define δW with the **opposite sign** (δW < 0
for stability). When reading such texts:

δW → −δW

Always verify the sign convention in the source before applying criteria.

**References**: Friedberg (1987) Ideal MHD, Freidberg (2014) Plasma Physics and
Fusion Energy, Wesson (2011) Tokamaks.
